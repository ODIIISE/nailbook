# F4: Mobile Conversion Optimization

## Findings

### [1] Users exposed to a progress indicator are willing to wait 3x longer and report higher satisfaction
- quote: "The people who saw the moving feedback bar experienced higher satisfaction and were willing to wait on average 3 times longer than those who did not see any progress indicators."
- url: https://www.nngroup.com/articles/progress-indicators/
- source_type: primary
- published: 2014-10-26
- confidence: high

### [2] The average cart/booking abandonment rate across 50 studies is 70.22%
- quote: "Based on the data we collected, we've calculated the average cart abandonment rate of 70.22%. This value is an average calculated based on 50 different studies containing statistics on ecommerce shopping cart abandonment."
- url: https://baymard.com/lists/cart-abandonment-rate
- source_type: primary
- published: 2025-09-22
- confidence: high

### [3] 17% of users abandon due to "too long / complicated checkout process" — but 20-60% of form fields can be eliminated
- quote: "Nearly 1 out of 5 shoppers have abandoned a cart in the last quarter due to a 'too long / complicated checkout process', yet for most checkouts it's possible to make a 20-60% reduction in the default number of form elements shown to users during checkout."
- url: https://baymard.com/blog/ecommerce-checkout-usability-report-and-benchmark
- source_type: primary
- published: 2025-02-02
- confidence: high

### [4] Number of form fields impacts checkout UX far more than number of steps; the average checkout has 11.3 fields but should have 8
- quote: "Our e-commerce UX benchmark and Premium research findings indicate that, as the number of form fields increase, the overall checkout UX performance decreases... most sites need only 8 form fields in total for a checkout flow — yet the average in 2024 was 11.3 form fields."
- url: https://baymard.com/blog/checkout-flow-average-form-fields
- source_type: primary
- published: 2024-06-26
- confidence: high

### [5] Splitting name into "First Name" / "Last Name" causes 42% of users to type their full name in the wrong field; a single "Full Name" field eliminates this
- quote: "42% of participants typed their full name in the 'First Name' field at least once... When testing sites with a 'Full Name' field, only 4% of users briefly hesitated."
- url: https://baymard.com/blog/checkout-flow-average-form-fields
- source_type: primary
- published: 2024-06-26
- confidence: high

### [6] Visible "Coupon Code" fields cause users to leave the site entirely to search for promo codes
- quote: "A handful of participants left the site to search for promo codes. In practice, as soon as users have left the checkout to go look for coupons, the chance of them not returning to complete the purchase increases significantly."
- url: https://baymard.com/blog/checkout-flow-average-form-fields
- source_type: primary
- published: 2024-06-26
- confidence: high

### [7] Delaying account creation to the confirmation step reduces abandonment; 84% of sites still force it during checkout
- quote: "During testing we observed that delaying the option to create an account until the Confirmation Step is a better-performing strategy... 84% of sites fail to do so."
- url: https://baymard.com/blog/checkout-flow-average-form-fields
- source_type: primary
- published: 2024-06-26
- confidence: high

### [8] Generic validation error messages cause users to abandon; adaptive error messages that describe the exact issue are used by only 7% of mobile sites
- quote: "Overly general error messages force the burden of determining what the problem is — and what needs to happen for the issue to be resolved — onto users... 93% of mobile sites don't use adaptive error messages."
- url: https://baymard.com/blog/mobile-ux-ecommerce
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026-07-14
- confidence: high

### [9] Loop progress indicators are best for 2-10 second waits; percent-done bars for 10s+ — static "Loading..." text should never be used
- quote: "Use a looped indicator for delays of 2-9 seconds and a percent-done indicator for delays of 10 seconds or more... Static progress indicators should be replaced with another type of indicator, because they do not offer enough information about what is happening."
- url: https://www.nngroup.com/articles/progress-indicators/
- source_type: primary
- published: 2014-10-26
- confidence: high

### [10] Better checkout design alone can yield a 35.26% average conversion rate increase
- quote: "The average large-sized ecommerce site can gain a 35.26% increase in conversion rate through better checkout design... This translates to $260 billion worth of lost orders which are recoverable solely through a better checkout flow & design."
- url: https://baymard.com/lists/cart-abandonment-rate
- source_type: primary
- published: 2025-09-22
- confidence: high

### [11] 40% of cart abandonment is caused by extra costs being too high — transparency on total cost up-front is critical
- quote: "40% Extra costs too high (shipping, tax, fees)... 12% I couldn't see / calculate total order cost up-front."
- url: https://baymard.com/lists/cart-abandonment-rate
- source_type: primary
- published: 2025-09-22
- confidence: high

### [12] Address validators prevent cascading delivery failures; 54% of mobile sites lack them
- quote: "Inaccurate addresses cause multiple, cascading issues... 54% of mobile sites don't provide an address validator."
- url: https://baymard.com/blog/mobile-ux-ecommerce
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026-07-14
- confidence: high
