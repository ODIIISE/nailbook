# F3: Auth & Checkout Optimization

## Findings

### [1] Guest checkout must be the most prominent option on the account-selection step — 47% of sites fail to do this
- quote: "47% of sites fail to make 'Guest Checkout' the most prominent option"
- url: https://baymard.com/blog/make-guest-checkout-prominent
- source_type: primary
- published: 2023-01-17
- confidence: high

### [2] 24% of US shoppers have abandoned a cart in the past quarter solely due to forced account creation
- quote: "24% of US internet shoppers have abandoned one or more shopping carts during the past quarter due solely to forced account creation"
- url: https://baymard.com/blog/make-guest-checkout-prominent
- source_type: primary
- published: 2023-01-17
- confidence: high

### [3] Delaying account creation to the confirmation step outperforms asking users at checkout start — 42% of sites fail to do this
- quote: "42% of sites interrupt users prior to or during the checkout flow to suggest they create an account"
- url: https://baymard.com/blog/delayed-account-creation
- source_type: primary
- published: 2023-09-19
- confidence: high

### [4] The average checkout flow contains 11.3 form fields — but only 8 are truly needed; 17% of users have abandoned due to checkout complexity
- quote: "the average checkout flow in 2024 is 5.1 steps long and contains 11.3 form fields — and 17% of users have abandoned due to checkout complexity"
- url: https://baymard.com/blog/checkout-flow-average-form-fields
- source_type: primary
- published: 2024-06-26
- confidence: high

### [5] 14% of online shoppers say they would never provide their phone number to an online store — requiring it without explanation causes abandonment
- quote: "14% of the respondents replied that they would '…never give an online store this information'"
- url: https://baymard.com/blog/explain-phone-number-field
- source_type: primary
- published: 2025-07-29
- confidence: high

### [6] 39% of e-commerce sites require a phone number during checkout without explaining why it's needed
- quote: "39% of sites in our ecommerce UX benchmark require users to provide their phone number without any sort of explanation"
- url: https://baymard.com/blog/explain-phone-number-field
- source_type: primary
- published: 2025-07-29
- confidence: high

### [7] Overly complex password requirements cause up to 19% checkout-abandonment rate among existing account users trying to sign in
- quote: "an unremembered password, followed by a password-reset process, caused a massive 18.75% average checkout abandonment rate"
- url: https://baymard.com/blog/password-requirements-and-password-reset
- source_type: primary
- published: 2022-11-29
- confidence: high

### [8] 82% of e-commerce sites have unnecessarily complex password requirements — only a 6–8 character minimum is recommended
- quote: "82% of sites in our ecommerce UX benchmark have unnecessarily complex password requirements"
- url: https://baymard.com/blog/password-requirements-and-password-reset
- source_type: primary
- published: 2022-11-29
- confidence: high

### [9] 70% of e-commerce users abandon their cart after adding items; better checkout UX can yield up to a 35% conversion rate increase
- quote: "70% of ecommerce users abandon their purchase after adding items to their cart" and "the average large-sized ecommerce site can gain as much as a 35% increase in conversion rate just by making design changes to their checkout process"
- url: https://baymard.com/blog/current-state-of-checkout-ux
- source_type: primary
- published: 2025-11-25
- confidence: high

### [10] 62% of sites still fail to make Guest Checkout the most prominent option (2025 updated figure, up from 47% in 2023)
- quote: "Our ecommerce UX benchmark reveals that 62% of sites fail to make 'Guest Checkout' the most prominent option"
- url: https://baymard.com/blog/current-state-of-checkout-ux
- source_type: primary
- published: 2025-11-25
- confidence: high

### [11] Guest checkout should be presented as a button, not a plain text link, and should appear above account sign-in options
- quote: "implementing this path as a button helps users locate 'Guest Checkout' while also making it easier to target — especially on touch interfaces"
- url: https://baymard.com/blog/make-guest-checkout-prominent
- source_type: primary
- published: 2023-01-17
- confidence: high

### [12] Sites should always allow guest checkout even for users whose email matches an existing account — forced sign-in after password issues guarantees abandonment
- quote: "users can always do a guest checkout, even if their email is already tied to an existing account"
- url: https://baymard.com/blog/password-requirements-and-password-reset
- source_type: primary
- published: 2022-11-29
- confidence: high

### [13] Using a single "Full Name" field instead of separate first/last name fields reduces friction — 42% of users type their full name into the "First Name" field when both fields are present
- quote: "42% of participants typed their full name in the 'First Name' field at least once"
- url: https://baymard.com/blog/checkout-flow-average-form-fields
- source_type: primary
- published: 2024-06-26
- confidence: high

### [14] Hiding "Address Line 2" behind a link prevents 30% of users from pausing in confusion; hiding coupon code fields prevents users from leaving the site to search for discounts
- quote: "30% of participants came to a stop when arriving at 'Address Line 2'" and "a handful of participants left the site to search for promo codes"
- url: https://baymard.com/blog/checkout-flow-average-form-fields
- source_type: primary
- published: 2024-06-26
- confidence: high

### [15] Users should be informed at the account-selection step that account creation will be available after checkout — reassures repeat customers without blocking the flow
- quote: "including a simple, short description for the guest checkout option at the account-selection step lets users who want to create an account know that they will have the option to create an account following checkout"
- url: https://baymard.com/blog/delayed-account-creation
- source_type: primary
- published: 2023-09-19
- confidence: high

## Dead ends
- Baymard Institute articles are behind a paywall for full Premium content; only free article excerpts were accessible via webfetch. However, the free articles contained sufficient quantitative data and specific claims.
- WebSearch tool was unavailable, limiting ability to find additional sources beyond direct URL fetching.
- Apple and Google HIG pages required JavaScript rendering and could not be fetched.
- Stripe blog and Twilio articles returned 404s — specific blog post URLs may have changed.
- No data found specifically on phone-based OTP authentication (as opposed to phone number collection during checkout forms). The research covers phone-as-form-field friction but not phone-as-auth-method (OTP/verification).
- No data found specifically on social login (Google/Apple/Facebook sign-in) adoption rates in booking apps.

## Suggested follow-ups
- Research phone-based OTP/verification code login patterns in Middle Eastern and Persian-first apps (e.g., Snapp, Tapsi, Digikala)
- Investigate social login adoption rates and conversion impact in beauty/wellness booking apps specifically
- Study one-tap phone authentication UX (e.g., Truecaller integration, phone number auto-detection) in emerging market mobile apps
