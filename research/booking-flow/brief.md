# Research Brief: Customer Booking Flow Improvement

**Date:** 2026-07-25
**Depth:** standard

## Current Flow

The NailBook customer booking flow has these steps:
1. Service selection (from landing page)
2. Addons selection (if service has addons)
3. Date selection (horizontal calendar strip)
4. Time slot selection (grid with suggested/available/booked states)
5. Auth (phone + PIN for existing users, or signup for new)
6. Confirmation + receipt

## Research Question

What are the best practices and leading patterns in appointment booking UX for beauty/salon apps? How do top-tier competitors (Fresha, Booksy, GlossGenius, Vagaro, etc.) handle:
- Service selection
- Date/time selection
- Auth/checkout flow
- Overall conversion optimization

## Scope

- **In:** Beauty/salon booking apps, mobile-first UX, conversion optimization, Persian/Iranian market considerations
- **Out:** Non-beauty booking (restaurants, healthcare), desktop-only patterns, backend/infrastructure

## Angles

1. **Competitor audit** — How do Fresha, Booksy, GlossGenius, Vagaro structure their booking flow?
2. **Date/time selection patterns** — What are the best calendar and time slot UI patterns?
3. **Auth & checkout optimization** — How to minimize friction in the auth step?
4. **Mobile-first conversion** — What micro-interactions and UI patterns increase booking completion?
5. **Iranian/Persian market** — Any region-specific UX considerations for salon booking?
