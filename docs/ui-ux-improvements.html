Forehand NailUI/UX UPGRADE PROPOSALS
01 / 05

Keeping the soul, sharpening the craft.

Eight concrete UI/UX upgrades to the current warm-paper design in production — same identity, less friction, more intention. Left phone reproduces today's build; right phone shows the proposal. Step through the journey with the tabs; numbered badges on the right phone map to the proposals below.

01
Hero that sells

The first fold works harder: live open-status merges into the CTA, and the next free slot is surfaced right under it.

02
Instant navigation

Sticky section chips (services · gallery · contact) so visitors jump anywhere without hunting.

03
Richer menu rows

Every service row gets an icon, a duration chip, the price, and a per-row book affordance.

04
Gallery with intent

Snappable look cards with name, price, and a quick “book this look” action on the card.

05
Quiet trust strip

A reassurance line computed from data the app already has — rating and bookings this month.

06
Persistent summary

Once a service is chosen, a pinned bar (service · total · edit) follows the user through steps 1–3.

07
Smarter time picking

Availability dots on the day strip, free-slot counts per group, and a one-tap “next available” quick select.

08
Friction-free finish

Progress checkmarks, auto-advancing OTP with a resend countdown, and calendar-add + code share on the receipt.
