FOREHAND NAIL · DESIGN SYSTEM 1.0

The Studio Design System

A warm, paper-toned visual language for the nail-booking experience — from the link-in-bio landing page to the moment a booking is confirmed. “Warm paper, espresso ink, and a single gold/rose accent family,” with purposeful motion that degrades cleanly under prefers-reduced-motion.

RTL · Persian-first Vazirmatn + Markazi Text Mobile-first · max-width 430px Reduced-motion aware
01

Design Principles

PRINCIPLES

These principles guide everyday decisions — every change should strengthen at least one of them.

1One primary actionBooking is the hero of the page; contact and navigation stay subordinate.
2Warm paperCream surfaces and soft corners evoke a beauty-salon feel.
3Real contentReal portfolio photos, prices, and availability carry the experience.
4Tactile feedbackPress, spring, and short animations — never sudden layout shifts.
5Accessible by defaultVisible focus, 44px+ targets, and reduced-motion support.
6One motion grammarA shared vocabulary: mask-up, breathe, fadeUp, and spring.

07

Two Surfaces, One Experience

SURFACES

The warm paper customer surface for the link-in-bio and booking flow; the monochrome system surface for admin panels. Both share the same motion grammar and click-to-copy token workflow, but each carries its own scale: warm paper uses the r1–r4 radius set (10/14/20/28px) with soft brown shadows, while the system surface uses the shadcn radius (10/14/18/24/32px) and neutral shadow scales.
